Infrastructure

Spinning Division

Constant modernization and technology upgrades make us a leading yarn manufacturer. Placements of machineries and spindles are well planned in strategic locations with economies of scale in mind.

  • No. of Spindles: 2,40,000
  • Comber / Draw Frame: LK 64, Reiter D50
  • Roving Machine / Ring Frame: LMW with autodoffers and linkconers
  • Compact Spinning: Suessen Elite
  • Autoconer: Schlafhorst 338, Murata 21 C, Muratec Q-PRO
  • Open End Spinning: Rieter BT923,3500 rotors
  • Yarn Clearers: Uster Quantum Siro 3
  • Product Range: Ne 30s to 120s Combed and Compact Single Yarn; Ne 12s to 30s count Open End Yarns

Weaving Division

Producing greige fabric tailored for the finest bedlinens available with the latest Picanol Omni Summum looms.

  • Looms: Picanol omni summum
  • Warping: Karl Meyer
  • Sizing: Karl Meyer Prosize
  • Production per year: 3 million meters
  • Thread Count: 100 TC to 1500 TC
  • Fabric Weaves: Plain, Twill, Drill, Sateen, Satin, Stripes, waffle, Rib stop, Herringbone, Swiss dot, Gabardine, and Oxford and Dobby design weaves
